# MicroSpends ~ Icarus Edition

**Precision Financial Ledger & Intelligence Suite**

MicroSpends Icarus is a high-fidelity, sleek personal finance application designed for absolute precision and aesthetic dominance. Featuring a liquid glass UI, real-time cloud synchronization, and AI-powered expense parsing, it transforms mundane ledgering into a high-density data visualization experience.

## ✨ Key Features

*   **Natural Language Entry**: Effortlessly log expenses using the Gemini-powered parser. Just speak or type "Coffee 250 Starbucks" and watch it materialize.
*   **Automatic Bank SMS Parsing**: Sync your incoming transaction alerts directly to your ledger with automated parsing and categorization.
*   **iOS Shortcuts Integration**: One-tap setup to bridge your physical banking notifications with your digital vault.
*   **Real-Time Cloud Persistence**: Securely synchronize your data across all devices using Google Cloud Firestore.
*   **High-Density Analytics**:
    *   **Spending Category Spheres**: Visualizing your financial distribution in a unique orbital layout.
    *   **Daily Spending Curve**: Track your outflow velocity with precision chronometer-style graphs.
*   **Wealth Oracle**: Objective wealth auditing and immediate action directives derived from real-time data.
*   **Subscription Vault**: Manage your recurring burn rate with detailed renewal tracking.
*   **Gamified Discipline**: Earn XP, level up, and achieve "Icarus Ascendant" status by maintaining financial discipline.
